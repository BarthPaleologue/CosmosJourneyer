# The physics of Cosmos Journeyer

The goal of this document is to present the rules of the universe for the game. More precisely, how they differ from our current understanding of our universe, and what would be the consequences on the people living inside Cosmos Journeyer's universe.

## Exotic matter for FTL

For gameplay purposes, warp-drives exist in the game and allow for FTL travel. How emerging time paradoxes are handled is still undecided.

In many FTL warp-drive related papers, they require negative energy density. One way of achieving such a thing is with a kind of "exotic matter" whose mass would be negative.

## Photon-only wormholes as a communication device

Exotic matter can also be used to make wormholes. Depending on a wormhole's geometry and physical properties, different forms of matter and radiation may be able to traverse it. In Cosmos Journeyer, artificial and natural wormholes only allow electromagnetic radiation (photons) to pass through.

This makes wormholes a practical FTL communication method. A worm hole can be created, then each character takes one of the ends of the worm-hole in a box to keep it stable, and can use it to send photons to the other, independent of distance. 

Those portable wormholes would probably get destroyed after having been used for a fixed budget of information/energy. This would make them useful, but also characters would not abuse them to avoid destroying them while away.

## Inter-universe wormholes

Since we are in worm-hole territory, we can also think about using them for inter-universe navigation. For example, black holes in the game could be one-way wormholes leading to another child universe (laws of physics might be slightly changed in these child universes).

This fact would fuel existential philosophy, by asking the question whether this universe is the base reality or not (orthogonal to the simulation hypothesis, that would also be an important question in universe).

Since photons can flow through wormholes in Cosmos Journeyer, transcendent beings would naturally wonder about the possibility of transferring themselves to one of the child universes to avoid the heat-death of the universe and entropy's final victory.

Having only photons going through wormholes opens the door for both matter production (protons and electrons) and matter manipulation (using electromagnetism). One could then imagine building a computer through a worm-hole, then sending its own consciousness to that computer, thus having moved between universes. That would be technologically be very hard to do. In game, transcendent beings would still be at the proof of concept stage, while organic beings would dismiss their effort as futile, joking how such hard problems would take longer than the remaining lifespan of the universe.

However having inter-universes wormholes being unidirectional creates uncertainty on the success of the computer construction. That would then be a leap of faith. The next logical step would be to practice the process inside the current universe using synthetic wormholes then once confidence is high enough, deploy the process to all known natural wormholes to maximize chances of success.

In Cosmos Journeyer, all black holes are unidirectional photon-only wormholes in disguise (inspired from spacetime diagrams of Kerr black holes featuring other space-time regions).

This can be a gameplay opportunity if the player chooses to transcend to explore other universes, with the limitation of uni-directionality.
